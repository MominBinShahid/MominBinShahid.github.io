Meem and monogram

Second round. Urdu as design material — م is the Urdu letter M, and its isolated form is already a logo: a bowl and a tail. Plus MBS monograms and M ideas that are not just the letter drawn plainly. No religious content anywhere; the script is used as a shape, nothing more.

Theme Colour

Urdu

09Meem
Meem

The isolated م, drawn geometrically. A circle and a tail — it was always a logo. Reads as an abstract mark to anyone who doesn't know the script.

10Seal
Seal

Meem set in a ring, like a stamp. Self-contained, so it needs no avatar frame anywhere it appears.

11Bilingual
Bilingual

A Latin M whose middle vertex is replaced by the meem's bowl. Two alphabets in one form. The most original mark in either round.

12Wordmark
مومن
Momin, in Nastaliq

Your name as a wordmark. Gorgeous large, illegible small — so it pairs with an icon rather than replacing one. Would be outlined to paths for production.

MBS

13Stack
Stack

Three letters in a column, monospaced so they align optically. Quiet and systematic. Works as a rail signature down the side of a page.

14Interlock
Interlock

M, B and S sharing one vertical stem. Proper monogram craft — the letters are dependent on each other rather than sitting side by side.

M, less obviously

15Unicursal
Unicursal

One unbroken line, never lifting. Handwritten rather than constructed — and it can draw itself on page load without looking gimmicky.

16Counter
Counter

The M exists only as the gap. Solid enough to hold a dark ground and read at favicon size — the strongest of the abstract set at 16px.

17Isometric
Isometric

An M with depth, seen slightly from above. Has real presence at large sizes; the shadow plane collapses below about 24px.

18Levels
Levels

Bar heights trace the M silhouette. Reads as audio levels — the agent listening to calls — and animates naturally as a loading state.

My picks: 11 Bilingual is the most original thing across both rounds — a Latin M holding an Urdu meem, legible to everyone, meaningful to you. 09 Meem is the most elegant and the best favicon. 16 Counter is the strongest purely abstract option.

12 Wordmark pairs rather than replaces — Nastaliq is beautiful at headline size and gone at 16px, so it would sit in a header or footer alongside an icon, not act as one.
