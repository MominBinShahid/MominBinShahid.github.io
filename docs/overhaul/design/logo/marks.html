Eight marks for Momin Bin Shahid

All drawn as plain SVG with a single stroke colour, so each one survives being printed in one ink, embossed, or rendered at 16 pixels. Source files are in docs/overhaul/design/logo/ — they export to PNG at any size.

Theme Colour
01Stacked
Stacked M

The letter reduced to four strokes. Reads instantly at any size. Safest of the eight — and the least distinctive.

02Signal
Signal

A waveform whose peaks spell an M. Ties to the agent work — machines listening. Gets muddy below about 20px.

03Caret
Caret

Two chevrons over a cursor rule. Reads as M and as a prompt at once. Developer-coded without a terminal cliché.

04Aperture
Aperture

Knocked out of a solid disc. The only one that works as an avatar without a container — it already is one.

05Ascent
Ascent

An M under a rising arc ending in a point. The career arc, full-stack into AI, without saying so.

06Matrix
Matrix

Departure-board dots. Warm, a little nostalgic, and it animates beautifully — dots can settle into place on load.

07Fold
Fold

Solid, letterpress-weight. The only one with real mass — holds up small and looks deliberate on a dark ground.

08Bracket
Bracket

Contained between brackets. The most literally "developer" of the set, which is either the point or the problem.

The 16px column is the real test. A mark that survives there works as a favicon, which is where a personal site's logo actually lives most of the time. 01, 04, 07 hold up best; 02 and 06 lose detail; 08 gets crowded.

Keeping 👨‍💻 is still an option. These aren't a replacement decision — the emoji could stay as the favicon while one of these becomes the site's own mark.
